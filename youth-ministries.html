SALT Teens

"Let no man despise they youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity." 1 Timothy 4:12

SALT Teens was established with the purpose of spiritually strengthening the youths from Years 7–12 through the knowledge of God's Word as well as to encourage them through fellowship by living godly and righteous lives so they can be effective witnesses for Christ.

Code of Conduct

Protecting the health & safety of our children.

Children are an important and valued part of our Church family. We take seriously the need to provide a safe and loving place for children to learn and play.

It is mandatory for anyone who holds a leadership role in Sydney Bible-Presbyterian Church or is involved with the care of children under 18 to adhere to our Code of Conduct.
