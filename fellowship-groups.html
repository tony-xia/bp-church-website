Ladies Bible Fellowship

"And let us consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works" Hebrews 10:24

The objective of the Ladies' Bible Fellowship (LBF) since its inception in 2003 has been to. cater to the spiritual needs of the more mature non-working ladies. Our focus us on things spiritual with occasional social outings for the purpose of fostering Christian fellowship.

All ladies are welcome to join us in the study of God's Word.

When
10:00am – 12:30pm Excluding school holidays.
Where
Church Fellowship Hall
